# 折线图趋势表达每日抄写

这份材料用于把折线图中最常用、最容易出错的表达练成自动化。目标不是积累大量“高级同义词”，而是在限时写作中稳定写出准确的主语、动词、介词、数值和时间。

## 每日抄写区

每天先抄下面20句。抄写时不要只抄字母，要同时说出句型的中文逻辑。

1. The figure stood at approximately 6,500 in 2011.
2. The number rose from 4,000 to 6,000 between 2012 and 2014.
3. The percentage fell sharply to around 40% in 2010.
4. The figure increased by approximately 2,000 over the period.
5. Shop openings declined considerably from 2011 to 2018.
6. The number of closures fluctuated between 5,000 and 7,000.
7. The figure remained stable at around 4,000 for three years.
8. It remained close to this level until 2017.
9. The proportion reached a peak of approximately 75% in 2020.
10. The figure peaked at around 75% in 2020.
11. It reached a low of approximately 500 in 2015.
12. The number bottomed out at around 500 before recovering.
13. It fell dramatically in 2015 before recovering to just over 5,000.
14. After declining to 40%, the figure rose steadily to 55%.
15. The two figures were similar, at around 30%.
16. Indonesia matched Thailand at approximately 30% in 2000.
17. Indonesia caught up with and then overtook the Philippines.
18. Malaysia rose sharply, while the Philippines declined gradually.
19. Although the figure fluctuated, it increased overall.
20. All four figures are predicted to rise further by 2040.

## 一、先固定主语

不要在写到一半时临时拼接 `opening shop`、`closed shop` 或 `all the metals prices`。先确定图表真正测量的指标。

| 图表指标 | 稳定表达 |
| --- | --- |
| 开店数量 | `the number of shop openings` / `the number of shops that opened` |
| 关店数量 | `the number of shop closures` / `the number of shops that closed` |
| 城市人口比例 | `the percentage/proportion of the population living in cities` |
| 某国的数据 | `the figure for Malaysia` / `Malaysia's figure` / `Malaysia` |
| 活动参与人数 | `participation in the activity` / `the number of participants` |
| 价格月度变化率 | `the monthly price change` / `the rate of price change` |

图表语境清楚时，可以直接用类别或国家作主语：

> Malaysia rose to approximately 75% in 2020.

> Nickel fell sharply in June.

不需要每句话都重复 `the figure for ...`，但第一次出现时应先把指标交代清楚。

## 二、`at`、`to`、`from` 和 `by`

这是目前最需要自动化的一组规则。

### `at`：表示处于某个数值

> The figure stood **at** 6,500.

> It remained stable **at** 4,000.

> The number peaked **at** 7,200.

### `to`：表示变化后的终点

> The figure rose **to** 6,000.

> It fell **to** 500.

> The number recovered **to** 5,000.

### `from A to B`：表示起点和终点

> Shop openings fell **from 8,500 to 4,000**.

### `by`：表示变化幅度，或截至某个时间

> The number fell **by 4,500**.

> The figure is predicted to reach 82% **by 2040**.

不要混淆：

> `fell to 4,000` = 降到了4,000

> `fell by 4,000` = 下降了4,000

## 三、上升、下降与恢复

### 动词结构

| 趋势 | 常用动词 | 过去式 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 上升 | `rise` | `rose` |
| 上升 | `increase` | `increased` |
| 增长 | `grow` | `grew` |
| 攀升 | `climb` | `climbed` |
| 下降 | `fall` | `fell` |
| 下降 | `decline` | `declined` |
| 下降 | `decrease` | `decreased` |
| 下跌 | `drop` | `dropped` |
| 回升 | `recover` | `recovered` |
| 反弹 | `rebound` | `rebounded` |

稳定句型：

> The figure **rose sharply to** 6,000.

> The number **declined gradually from** 8,000 **to** 5,000.

> It **fell dramatically before recovering to** 5,000.

### 名词结构

> The figure experienced **a dramatic increase**.

> There was **a sharp decline** in the number of openings.

注意词性：

> `a dramatic increase`：形容词＋名词

> `increased dramatically`：动词＋副词

## 四、稳定与波动

### 稳定

> The figure remained stable at around 4,000.

> The number changed relatively little over the period.

> It remained close to this level until 2017.

`constant` 表示几乎完全不变，比 `stable` 更绝对。数据有轻微变化时优先用 `stable`、`relatively steady` 或 `close to this level`。

### 波动

> The figure fluctuated between 5,000 and 7,000.

> Despite some fluctuations, the number declined overall.

> The figure showed considerable fluctuations throughout the period.

不要把“变化率下降”自动写成“价格下降”。当图中数值仍为正时，价格可能仍在上涨，只是涨幅缩小。

## 五、最高点、最低点和交点

### 最高点

> The figure reached a peak of 7,200 in 2013.

> The number peaked at 7,200 in 2013.

### 最低点

> The figure reached a low of approximately 500 in 2015.

> The number bottomed out at around 500 in 2015.

如果只是某一阶段的低点，不要写成全时期最低点：

> After peaking in 1990, the figure fell to a subsequent low in 2010.

### 相同、追平和超过

> The two figures were equal at around 30%.

> Indonesia matched Thailand at approximately 30%.

> Indonesia caught up with the Philippines in 2010.

> Table tennis overtook martial arts by 2015.

只写能够体现重要排名变化或趋势分化的交点，不必报告每一次相交。

## 六、时间表达

| 作用 | 表达 |
| --- | --- |
| 从A年到B年 | `from 2011 to 2018` |
| A年与B年之间 | `between 2011 and 2018` |
| 截至某年 | `by 2040` |
| 此后 | `thereafter` / `subsequently` |
| 次年 | `in the following year` |
| 随后20年 | `over the following two decades` |
| 预测期内 | `during the forecast period` |
| 直到某年 | `until 2017` |

不要写：

- `from 2011 and 2018`
- `until by 2014`
- `after the year`
- `in the later 20 years`
- `after the 2010`

## 七、预测数据

历史数据和预测数据需要区分时态：

> The figure **rose** to 75% in 2020.

> It **is predicted to reach** 82% by 2040.

常用结构：

- `is predicted to reach ...`
- `is expected to rise ...`
- `is projected to increase ...`
- `a projected figure of ...`
- `a projected 56%`

`projected` 表示“预测的、预计的”：

> The figure recovered steadily to **a projected 56%**.

## 八、连接趋势

### 相反趋势：`while` / `whereas`

> Malaysia rose sharply, while the Philippines declined.

> Openings fell, whereas closures remained relatively stable.

### 让步：`although`

> Although the figure declined initially, it increased overall.

`although` 不是单纯表示两个对象不同，而是表示“尽管A，B仍然成立”。

### 相似趋势：`similarly`

> Copper declined during the first half of the year. Similarly, zinc fell before recovering.

### 补充结果：分词结构

> The figure rose steadily, reaching 6,000 in 2014.

> Indonesia increased dramatically, matching Thailand in 2000.

## 九、把多个节点压缩成变化链

不需要为每一个数据点单独造句。保留起点、转折点和终点：

> The figure fell from 6% in January to 1% in March before reaching a low of −3% in June.

> It then recovered to −1%, remained stable for three months, and ended the year at 1%.

常用骨架：

> `fell from A to B before reaching C`

> `rose to A, remained there until B, and ended at C`

> `after reaching A, the figure declined to B before recovering to C`

## 十、Overview 的稳定原则

Overview 写结论，不写证明结论的全部数据。优先寻找：

1. 共同趋势；
2. 最高或最低；
3. 最大增幅或降幅；
4. 明显波动或异常点；
5. 重要排名变化。

可用结构：

> Overall, all four figures increased, although the rate of growth varied considerably.

> Overall, openings declined considerably, while closures fluctuated and finished slightly below their initial level.

> Overall, Malaysia recorded the highest final figure, whereas Indonesia experienced the most substantial growth.

Overview 和 Detail 可以使用不同的分组标准。Overview 负责全图结论，Detail 负责用最方便的方式组织证据。

## 十一、个人高频错误对照

| 不稳定表达 | 稳定表达 |
| --- | --- |
| `the number for opening shop` | `the number of shop openings` |
| `the number for closed shop` | `the number of shop closures` |
| `declined at 4,000` | `declined to 4,000` |
| `climbed at 5,000` | `climbed to 5,000` |
| `stood 20%` | `stood at 20%` |
| `fell back 2012` | `fell back to its 2012 level` |
| `experienced dramatically increase` | `experienced a dramatic increase` |
| `a relatively fluctuation` | `some fluctuation` / `relative fluctuations` |
| `a sharp upward` | `a sharp upward trend` / `rose sharply` |
| `started the lowest` | `had the lowest initial figure` |
| `with the same value of Thailand` | `matching Thailand` / `with its figure matching that of Thailand` |
| `will be have an increase` | `is predicted to increase` |

## 十二、写完后的两分钟检查

按这个顺序检查，不要漫无目的地重读：

1. 主语：写的是正确指标吗？
2. 数据：年份、数值、正负和预测是否正确？
3. 介词：静态用 `at`，动态终点用 `to`，幅度用 `by`。
4. 时态：历史用过去时，预测用 `is predicted/expected to`。
5. 词形：`dramatic increase` 还是 `increased dramatically`？
6. 句子：`whereas`、`although` 后面是否有完整主句？
7. 拼写：国家名、`approximately`、`percentage` 是否正确？
8. 字数：是否达到150词？

## 每日练习方法

1. 抄写“每日抄写区”的20句，并朗读一遍。
2. 从当天句子中任选5句，把主语、年份和数字替换成已经做过的题目数据。
3. 遮住原句，凭记忆写出其中3句。
4. 最后只检查 `at / to / by` 和动词过去式。

连续练习的目标不是一字不差地背诵，而是看到图表后能直接写出正确结构，不再现场拼装语法。
