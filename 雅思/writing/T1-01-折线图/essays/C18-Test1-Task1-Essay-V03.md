## Essay

The graph illustrates how the percentage of the population living in cities in four Asian countries changed from 1970 to 2020, with predictions for 2030 and 2040.

Overall, the urban population figures for Malaysia and Indonesia increased dramatically over the historical period. By contrast, the Philippines and Thailand experienced more modest overall growth, although the former fluctuated. All four figures are predicted to rise further by 2040.

The figures for the Philippines and Malaysia both stood at around 30% in 1970. By 1990, they had risen to similar levels of just under 50%. Malaysia then rose sharply to approximately 75% in 2020 and is predicted to peak at around 82% by 2040, while the figure for the Philippines declined to approximately 43% in 2010 before recovering steadily to a projected 56%.

Thailand stood at just under 20% in 1970 and rose gradually to slightly over 30% by 2020. It is then predicted to increase more rapidly, reaching approximately 50% in 2040. Indonesia had the lowest initial figure, at around 13%, but increased dramatically, matching Thailand at approximately 30% in 2000 and the Philippines at just over 40% in 2010. It subsequently rose to about 52% in 2020 and is expected to reach approximately 64% by 2040.

## Revision notes

- 后续独立写作时继续使用“Overview 负责结论，Detail 负责证据”的原则。
- Overview 和 Detail 可以采用不同的分组标准，只要正文数据能够证明总体结论。
- 相近数据可以用 `around`、`approximately`、`just under` 和 `just over` 合并，但不要用过于精确的单一数字覆盖并不相同的数据。
- 优先确保数据、介词、冠词、词形和预测时态准确，再考虑词汇替换和复杂句式。
