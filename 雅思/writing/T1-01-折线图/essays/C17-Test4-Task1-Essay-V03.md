### Clarifications from this revision

#### `In the following year` 的指向

原文主句先给出 `in the first four years`，即2011—2014年；`with a peak ... in 2013` 是伴随补充。因此，后面的 `In the following year` 可以承接“前四年”这一完整时期，指2015年。此前将它机械地理解为2013年之后的2014年过于严格，该批评已经撤回。

为了兼顾连贯性和零歧义，修订范文使用：

> After this initial four-year period, ... before recovering ... the following year.

这样无需连续写 `In 2015 / In 2016`，也不会让读者寻找参照点。

#### Overview 中“总体下降”和“波动”的区别

`openings fluctuated much more than closures` 中的 `more` 可能指幅度或频率，但两种解释都缺乏明确数据支持。更可靠的特征是 openings 从约8,500降至3,000，总体降幅明显；closures 则出现了最突出的单次骤降。

修订稿尽量保留用户原有框架：

> Overall, both shop openings and closures fluctuated considerably, although openings declined more noticeably over the period. Notably, closures experienced a sharp temporary fall in 2015.

可迁移的应当是“共同趋势 → 主要区别 → 特别特征”的骨架；每道题填入的具体特征仍应由图表决定。

#### `however` 的逗号

下面的插入语结构正确：

> In the following year, however, the figure declined.

去掉 `however` 后仍是完整句子：

> In the following year, the figure declined.

但原 Overview 用一个逗号连接两个独立句子：

> ..., however, the number of shop openings...

应改为句号、分号，或使用从属连词：

> Both figures fluctuated considerably; however, openings declined more noticeably.

> Both figures fluctuated considerably, although openings declined more noticeably.

#### 比较中的省略

为了避免重复 `the number of shop closures`，可以使用：

> The number of shop openings fluctuated more than that of shop closures.

`that` 代替 `the number`，使比较对象保持平行。不过本图不建议比较谁“波动更多”，应比较谁的总体降幅更明显。

#### 如何引出2015年的异常

`On the other hand` 更适合引出另一种观点或明显对立面。本句只是补充值得注意的异常特征，建议使用：

- `Notably, ...`
- `Most notably, ...`
- `In particular, ...`
- `A particularly striking feature was ...`

本篇最简洁的是：

> Notably, closures experienced a sharp temporary fall in 2015.

#### `went through slight fluctuations`

原文的错误是副词直接修饰名词：

> `slightly fluctuations`

改成下面的结构后语法成立，可以保留以避免机械重复：

> `went through slight fluctuations`

其他自然选择包括 `experienced slight fluctuations`、`fluctuated slightly` 和 `remained relatively stable`。不必为了避免重复而强制更换已经准确的核心词。
