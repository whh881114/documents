## Essay
The pie charts compare the proportions of three nutrients contained in four typical meals consumed in the USA.

Overall, sodium and saturated fat showed similar patterns, with their largest proportions being consumed at dinner. By contrast, added sugar was mainly consumed in snacks. The proportions of all three nutrients consumed at breakfast were relatively low.

Regarding sodium and saturated fat, dinner represents 43% of sodium consumption and 37% of saturated fat intake, making it the largest contributor in both cases. Lunch ranks second, accounting for 29% and 26% respectively. Breakfast contains relatively small proportions of these two nutrients, at 14% for sodium and 16% for saturated fat. Snacks also make up 14% of sodium intake, although their share of saturated fat is slightly higher, at 21%.

A different pattern can be seen in the consumption of added sugar. Snacks are responsible for 42% of the total, almost twice the figure for dinner, at 23%. Lunch contributes a further 19%, while breakfast once again records the lowest proportion, at just 16%.
