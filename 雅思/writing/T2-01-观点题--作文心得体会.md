# T2-01-观点题--作文心得体会


## INTRODUCTION
  - 题目讨论什么？
    - `Access to clean water is a basic human right. Therefore every home should have a water supply that is provided free of charge.`
    - 第一步还是要改写题目，其实是考核考生是否看懂了题目。比如看懂了背景，讨论的对象是什么。
      - 前提`Access to clean water is a basic human right.`。
      - 讨论的点，`Therefore every home should have a water supply that is provided free of charge.`。
      
  - 我的立场是什么？
    - 我的看法是yes/no，二选一，不要搞to some extent，要坚持自己的立场，这样会更加focus一点，这样结构简单，整体内容也更紧凑。

  - 示例：`Access to clean water is undoubtedly essential for human health and dignity. However, I completely disagree 
    that every household should receive its water supply free of charge. `
