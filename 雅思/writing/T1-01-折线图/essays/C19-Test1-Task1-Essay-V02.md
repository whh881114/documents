## Essay
The graph shows how the numbers of participants changed across different activities at one social centre in Melbourne, Australia, from 2000 to 2020.

Overall, participation in the film club and martial arts remained relatively stable, with the film club attracting the most participants throughout the period. Table tennis and musical performances both saw increases in participation, particularly table tennis. In contrast, participation in amateur dramatics declined overall.

The film club attracted around 65 participants in 2000, compared with approximately 35 for martial arts. Film club attendance declined slightly to 60 in 2010 before recovering to about 66 by 2020. Meanwhile, the figure for martial arts fluctuated between roughly 32 and 38, finishing close to its starting level.

Table tennis participation increased from around 16 to 20 between 2000 and 2005, then remained unchanged until 2010. It subsequently rose sharply to approximately 54 in 2020, overtaking martial arts by 2015 to become the second most popular activity. Musical performances also gained participants, rising from zero in 2005 to about 18 in 2020. By contrast, amateur dramatics initially increased slightly from around 26 to 28 participants, but then declined steadily to just 6. By 2015, it had fallen below musical performances.
