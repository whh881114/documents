#### 1. `increase` 和 `decrease` 可以作名词吗？

可以。`increase` 和 `decrease` 本身都可以作动词或名词，不需要添加 `-ment`：

- 动词：`The population increased.`
- 名词：`There was an increase in the population.`
- 动词：`The percentage decreased.`
- 名词：`The city experienced a decrease in its population.`

描述趋势时，可以成对记忆以下结构：

| 动词结构 | 名词结构 |
| --- | --- |
| `increase dramatically` | `a dramatic increase` |
| `decrease considerably` | `a considerable decrease` |
| `grow significantly` | `significant growth` |
| `decline slightly` | `a slight decline` |

因此：

- `has a dramatic increasement` 应改为 `increased dramatically` 或 `experienced a dramatic increase`
- `experienced considerable decreasement` 应改为 `experienced a considerable decrease` 或 `decreased considerably`

不要根据中文“增加／增加量”的区别，凭感觉给英文动词添加 `-ment`。遇到不确定的词，优先使用已经确认过的基本词形。

#### 4. 怎样自然地进入下一个比较？

不需要在每句话前使用新的连接词。完成一类比较后，可以用一句主题句明确切换指标：

> Turning to their shares of the total population, the two groups followed opposite trends.

随后按照年份推进：

> Manhattan accounted for 76% of the total in 1800, compared with 24% for the other districts. By 1900, the gap had narrowed considerably, as the corresponding figures stood at 54% and 46%. In 2000, however, the situation had reversed, with the respective shares reaching 19% and 81%.

这段的路线是：

```text
切换到占比
→ 1800 年的起点
→ 1900 年的差距缩小
→ 2000 年的地位反转
```

可复用的段落衔接表达：

- 从一个对象转向另一个对象：`By contrast, the combined population of the other districts increased throughout the period.`
- 从人口数量转向占比：`Turning to their shares of the total population, the two groups followed opposite trends.`
- 进入中间年份：`By 1900, the gap had narrowed considerably.`
- 进入最终年份：`In 2000, however, the situation had reversed.`

连贯不等于每句话都必须使用连接词。还可以通过时间顺序、代词指代、相同指标和句子之间的逻辑关系实现衔接。

下一版可以记住：

> **先分指标，再按时间走，最后指出差距或反转。**
