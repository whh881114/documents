## Essay
The line graph shows how participation in five activities at a social center in Melbourne changed between 2000 and 2020.

Overall, participation in the film club and martial arts remained relatively stable, with the former being the most popular activity throughout the period. By contrast, table tennis and musical performances became more popular, while amateur dramatics experienced a considerable decline.

Film-club participation declined slightly from approximately 64 in 2000 to 60 in 2010, before recovering to about 66 by 2020. The figure for martial arts fluctuated between roughly 32 and 38 participants over the period and finished at around 36, the same level as at the beginning.

Table-tennis participation rose from approximately 16 in 2000 to 20 in 2005 and remained unchanged in 2010. At that point, it was equal to the figure for amateur dramatics; thereafter, however, the two activities followed sharply contrasting trends. Table tennis climbed rapidly to around 35 participants in 2015 and 54 in 2020, whereas amateur dramatics fell from 20 to approximately 6 over the same period. Meanwhile, participation in musical performances increased from zero in 2005 to around 12 in 2010 and 18 by 2020.
