## Essay
The line graph shows the average monthly precentage change in the prices of copper, nickel and zinc in 2014.

Overall, the monthly price changes for all three metals generally declined during the first halft of the year, reaching their lowest levels by June. They then remained relatively stable for several months before recoving towards the end of the year.

Nickel experienced the most dramtic changes, with its monthly figure falling from 6% in January to 1% in March and April before reaching a low of -3% in June. It then recovered to -1%, where it remained from July to September, before ending the year at 1%.

Copper showed a gentler downward trend, falling from 2% in January to -0.5% in May and June. It subsequently rose to 1% and remained at this level until September, finishing at approximately 1.5% in December. Similarly, the figure for zinc dropped from a peak of 3% in February to -1% in June. After remaining slightly negative until October, it rebounded to 2% by the end of the year.
