### Task Achievement

Overview 的方向比 V01 更好：已经意识到两项数据结束时都低于起点，而且 openings 的总体降幅更大。但下面这句需要修正：

> both figures for shop closures and openings declined over the period

`declined over the period` 容易让人理解为持续下降，而 closures 实际上剧烈波动。更精确的是：

> both figures ended lower than they began

下面这句成功选中了2015年的最大异常，但表达需要修改：

> there is a dramatic decrease number of shop closures in 2015

可以写成：

> shop closures experienced a dramatic decrease in 2015

或者在不写具体年份的 Overview 中概括为：

> shop closures showed a sharp temporary drop before recovering rapidly

Detail 中最严重的数据问题是：

> the lowest around 7000

2015年的 closures 约为500—700，绝不是7,000。可以安全地写成 `below 1,000`，避免读取图片时过度精确。这个错误相差一个数量级，会直接影响 Task Achievement。

此外，前一句刚写2013年达到峰值，`In the following year` 指的是2014年，但最低点发生在2015年。这里应写 `It then declined ... in 2015`，不要使用会造成错误年份关系的 `the following year`。

### Coherence and Cohesion

文章继续按 openings 和 closures 分成两个 Detail 段，结构清楚，不需要改变。

第一段的时间线已经比较顺畅：

目前主要问题是独立分句经常只用逗号连接。例如：

可以保留为一句，但需使用正确动词并清楚连接：

> In 2015, it fell back to its 2012 level and remained close to this figure until 2017, before ending the period at 3,000.

### Lexical Resource

主要拼写和搭配问题：

- `constrat` → `contrast`
- `dropped much greater than closures` → `showed a much greater decline than closures`
- `a dramatic decrease number` → `a dramatic decrease in the number`
- `it backed to` → `it fell back to` / `it returned to`
- `ended at the lowest 3000` → `ended at a low of 3,000`
- `remained some fluctuations` → `fluctuated` / `showed some fluctuations`
- `declined to the lowest around 700` → `declined to a low of around 700`
- `stayed slightly fluctuations` → `fluctuated slightly` / `remained relatively stable`

需要特别固定词性：

- `fluctuated slightly`：动词＋副词
- `showed slight fluctuations`：动词＋形容词＋复数名词
- `remained relatively stable`：系动词＋副词＋形容词

不能混合成 `stayed slightly fluctuations`。

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

Overview 第一处使用了逗号连接两个完整句子：

> ..., however, number of shop openings ...

可以改为：

> ..., although the number of shop openings showed a much greater decline.

或者拆开：

> Both figures ended lower than they began. However, the number of shop openings showed a much greater decline.

其他主要问题：

- `number of shop openings` 前需要 `the`。
- `much greater` 不能直接修饰动词 `dropped`；应使用 `dropped much more sharply`，或改成名词结构 `showed a much greater decline`。
- `there is` 与历史图表的时态不一致，应使用 `there was`；但本句最好直接以 `shop closures` 作主语。
- `it backed` 不能表示数据回落；应使用 `it fell back`。
- `remain/stay` 后接形容词：`remained stable`；`fluctuate` 本身是动词：`the figure fluctuated`。
- 数字建议使用千位逗号：`8,500`、`4,000`、`6,100`。
